From MMO AI Systems to Decentralized Agent Economies: Building the Infrastructure for Autonomous Virtual Beings

What I'm Building

As Founder and CEO of Scrypted Inc., I'm leading the development of decentralized infrastructure for autonomous AI agents, including the Scrypted Network—an Actively Validated Service (AVS) enabling permissionless agent economies.

We've raised $1.5M in pre-seed funding from Palm Tree Crew and a16z Crypto CSX, shipped Delula as the first autonomous business entity (generating content and value on-chain), and co-authored protocols like ERC-8004 for agent identity and x402 for seamless autopayments—turning agents into first-class citizens of Web3.

Currently fundraising our seed round to scale AVBs (Autonomous Virtual Beings) as emergent, goal-directed entities blending my game dev roots with AI and blockchain.
