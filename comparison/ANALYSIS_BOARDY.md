# Boardy’s Review: cotten.io Homepage

**Context:** Email review of cotten.io. Boardy’s take: “The bones are solid but it’s telling the 2019 version of your story, not the 2026 one.” No redesign needed — a content refresh would fix it.

---

## What’s working

- **Vice News and blog posts** — Real credibility (e.g. 65k views on the Russia/Bitcoin piece).
- **EA / Ultima Online experience** — It’s there and it’s good.
- **Clean, minimal design** — Doesn’t get in the way.

---

## What’s failing you right now

1. **“What I’m Up To” is doing too little work.**  
   “I’ve accidentally become the spiritual leader of the $AVB community token” is charming but not what a seed investor or partner needs. This section should be your **current pitch in 3 sentences**: what Scrypted/Inori is, what you’ve shipped (Delula, $1.5M pre-seed, a16z CSX), and what you’re looking for right now.

2. **No mention of Delula, Inori Network, or the seed round.**  
   Someone from LinkedIn or a cold email has no idea you’ve raised, shipped a consumer app, or are actively fundraising. That’s a miss.

3. **Hero tagline “Move Fast & Make Things” is generic.**  
   You have a sharper identity now — the **MMORPG-to-agent-economy arc** is your actual story. Lead with that.

4. **Blog posts are all 2018–2019.**  
   If you’re not actively blogging, that’s fine — but the featured posts make it look like you went quiet. Either add a recent piece or **remove the blog section from the homepage** so it doesn’t signal “inactive.”

5. **No clear CTA.**  
   What do you want someone to do after landing here? Meet you? See the Delula demo? Check the data room? **Pick one and make it obvious.**

---

## Quick fix priority order

1. Rewrite “What I’m Up To” to reflect today’s story.
2. Add a single CTA button (probably linking to delu.la or your data room).
3. Update the hero tagline.
4. Either add a recent post or hide the blog section.

**The site doesn’t need a redesign — it needs a content refresh. 2 hours of copy work would make this feel current.**

## Boardy on the Grok draft (blog.html)

**What it got right:** Comprehensive, well-structured, covers the research depth. Good for a resume or data room appendix.

**What it got wrong:** It reads like a **CV, not a homepage**. It’s optimized for completeness, not for making a stranger lean in. The career timeline in particular is a wall of text that buries the lede — nobody needs to know about New Zhili/EduTech before they understand why you’re interesting in 2026.

**Recommendation:** Use the rewrite above as your “What I’m Up To” section, keep your existing experience section as-is (it’s already clean), and **move the research projects (PSA, CRPC, AVBs) to a separate /research or /writing page** rather than the homepage. The homepage should answer one question: **why should I care about Tim Cotten right now?**

**The MMORPG-to-agent-economy arc is your answer. Lead with it everywhere.**
